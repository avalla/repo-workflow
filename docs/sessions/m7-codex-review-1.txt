- [P2] Require merge approval for every merge candidate — /Users/andrea/dev/tmp/repo-workflow/schemas/config.schema.json:653-668
  A merge-mode config passes validation when its required `merge` approval applies only to `release`, but `inspect` then reports `project/merge` as applicable and `project/approval-merge` as inapplicable for a feature. This leaves the effective gate report inconsistent with the requirement for recorded approval on every merge. Reject selectors that can exclude a merge candidate, or make the merge approval inherit the merge gate’s applicability ([ADR 0006](docs/adr/0006-configurable-delivery-integration.md#L22-L24)).
- [P2] Open the PR before waiting on PR-triggered CI — /Users/andrea/dev/tmp/repo-workflow/skills/integrate-delivery/SKILL.md:17-23
  For projects whose required CI runs on `pull_request`, step 2 asks the integrator to verify CI for the new head before step 3 pushes that head and opens the PR. Those checks cannot run in that order, and earlier results cannot substitute for the new head’s checks. Separate local verification from remote verification: push and open the PR when authorized, then wait for checks on that exact head before closing the PR gate or enabling auto-merge ([core/POLICY.md](core/POLICY.md#L25-L30)).

I verified that the update applies successfully but `doctor` then reports `task-ledger-missing` as BLOCKED. Include the new ledger in the reviewed update plan when absent, while preserving any existing ledger ([docs/USAGE.md](docs/USAGE.md#L33-L36)).
