- [P2] Verify that the merge queue honors the configured method — /Users/andrea/dev/tmp/repo-workflow/skills/integrate-delivery/SKILL.md:23-25
  On a GitHub branch that requires a merge queue, `gh pr merge` warns that the queue controls the merge strategy and proceeds even if the caller supplied a strategy.
